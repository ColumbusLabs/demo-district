Demo District

A walkable showcase for things people build with AI. This preview uses sample storefronts; nothing external loads unless you choose it.

Keyboard and mouse: WASD to walk, drag or arrow keys to look, Enter to open the storefront you face, M for the map. Escape releases the scene; Tab reaches these controls.

Touch: drag the scene to look, use the stick to walk, tap a storefront to open it.
